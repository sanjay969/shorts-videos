## Pillar 1: Shorts engine (3 per day, 6:00 / 7:30 / 9:00 PM ET, one at a time)
Daily mix:
- **Slot 1 (6:00 PM): "What Happens If You…" body/science series.** The only format Studio rates as bringing viewers back. Same title start, same look, same sign-off every day.
- **Slot 2 (7:30 PM): strange true US story or US mystery**, lesser-known cases preferred.
- **Slot 3 (9:00 PM): Part 1 of a two-part story, OR the trailer Short for a long video.** A Part 1 ends on a real cliffhanger + "Part 2 tomorrow at 6 PM, follow so you don't miss it". The next day's Slot 2 is Part 2. Part 1/Part 2 is the main tool for returning viewers and subscribers.

Rules: 25–30 s, first 2 s = strangest fact + number, payoff in the last second loops back to the start, one spoken sign-off "Follow for one strange true story every night", English only, US units, 2–3 on-topic hashtags + #shorts, no off-niche topics (business, India, internet lore). Never flush a backlog; drop the weakest Short instead.

## Pillar 2: Subscriber conversion + returning viewers
- Same sign-off line and visual end card on every Short.
- Two-part stories every day (see Slot 3).
- Playlists (automatic once the token has write scope): "What Happens If You…", "Unsolved American Mysteries", "Strange True History"; every new Short goes into its playlist.
- Channel About text and keywords focused on the US mystery / what-if niche (queued).
- Reply to real comments within a day (daily analysis job), short and friendly, ending with a question that invites another reply.

## Pillar 3: Long-form bridge (2 per week: Sunday + Wednesday)
- Fewer, better longs: every long is made from a topic whose Short already beat the median, so there is a proven audience to send over.
- Search-first title ("What Really Happened to…?"), lesser-known US case, thumbnail rules from the long job.
- Each long gets a trailer Short (Slot 3 the next day). Sanjay sets "Related video" on the trailer + original topic Short in Studio. This is the one manual step and it matters most.
- Success check after 7 days: impressions > 1,000 and views from Search + Shorts > 0. If two longs in a row fail this, change the topic type.

## Pillar 4: Test and learn (weekly)
- Each week test ONE thing on the Shorts (title style, hook type, length 20 vs 30 s, sign-off wording); keep the winner, log it in analysis-log.md.
- Every Sunday the daily analysis job updates the scoreboard below, compares with the targets and changes at most 2 rules in insights.md.
- Any Short with under 300 views after 7 days that isn't a WIN: retitle once (data reason), never re-upload.
